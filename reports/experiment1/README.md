# Experiment 1 — Final report

This is the primary reading path for the preserved preprocessing and model
feasibility study. Read the chapters in order:

1. [Features](01_features.md): definitions, preprocessing, and measurement caveats.
2. [Data, sessions, and windows](02_data_sessions_and_windows.md): corpus, split,
   labels, augmentation, and exclusions.
3. [Experiments and models](03_experiments_and_models.md): comparisons, selection,
   complete results, and interpretation.
4. [Selected models and conclusions](04_selected_models_and_conclusions.md):
   finalists, limitations, provenance, and conclusions.

`tables/` and `figures/` hold the evidence used by these chapters. The documents
under `archive/` are earlier supporting references, outside the main reading path.
Their historical statements about tracking and development status may predate
this snapshot.

See the [reproduction guide](REPRODUCING.md) for prerequisites and commands,
[study inventory](../../study/README.md) for original run artifacts, and
[scientific protocol](../../study/plans/experiment_plan.md) for planned methods.
The [engineering phase plan](../../plans/experiment_1_phases.md) explains phases
A–E; CLI phases 3–6 instead refer to study stages.
